> Key differences from the old model:
>
> | Old (v1.0) | New (v2.0) |
> |-----------|-----------|
> | Single weight: `W = leaf_count × F` | Multi-dim: `OwnScore = W × C × D` |
> | Blast radius = `OwnScore / suite_total` | FailureCost includes correlated failures |
> | Suite total: 1107 pts | Suite total: 3001 pts |
> | Gate: no scenario > 10% blast radius | Zero-tolerance pillars + 10%/5% score gates |
> | No fix-priority output | Agent JSON `fix_queue` sorted by FailureCost desc |
